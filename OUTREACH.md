## Notes on using these

- **Don't send the PDF cold.** It's an attachment for people who already
  replied, or a handout at a market. The link does the work in an email.
- **The AI phone agent is not in the vendor pitch on purpose.** Sell it on the
  call, and only to people who take orders or bookings by phone — caterers,
  bakeries, food trucks. Offering it to everyone makes the whole thing sound
  like an upsell.
- **Capacity is the real limit.** Care plans are a standing weekly obligation on
  top of a full-time job; the $250 season change is the product to push, since
  it batches the same work into two predictable weeks a year.
